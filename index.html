Frequently Asked Questions about CDs

APR (Annual Percentage Rate) is the simple interest rate before compounding is considered. APY (Annual Percentage Yield) reflects the true annual return earned on your money when interest is compounded daily or monthly. APY is always slightly higher than APR.
A CD Ladder involves splitting your total savings across multiple CDs of varying maturities (e.g., 6 months, 1 year, 2 years, 3 years). As each CD matures, you can either cash out penalty-free or roll it into a new top-rate CD, providing regular liquidity while capturing higher long-term yields.
Most banks impose an early withdrawal penalty if you break a standard CD before its term ends—typically between 3 to 6 months of interest. However, your initial principal deposit is protected by law. No-penalty CDs allow penalty-free withdrawals at the cost of slightly lower APY.
